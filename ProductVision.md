# Product Visison 

For independent clothing brands and shoppers who desire style tailored to their taste. “ClothingPlace” is a local marketplace that helps people discover and shop from small clothing brands near them matched to their personal style. Unlike apps like Etsy or Depop, ClothingPlace focuses on user locality, rather than a global, search-driven functionality. Our product provides visibility for small brands and provides shoppers with style-based recommendations, surfacing clothing and brands you would have previously never seen.

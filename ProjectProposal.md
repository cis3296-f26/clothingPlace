# Project Proposal: ClothingPlace

## Overview

Often, small clothing businesses have difficulty promoting and marketing their brand so e-commerce customers can see and shop from them. ClothingPlace would close that gap between the customer and the business. It will streamline a central area where customers can access a variety of clothing brands, providing a marketplace that helps people discover small clothing brands near them.

## Personal Style Recommendations

A style-based recommendation feed surfaces brands customers wouldn't find on their own, matched to a personal style profile. To get started, users will be offered a style-based quiz at signup to kickstart the style profile and the recommendation feed to match items to the user's style.

## Cart and Checkout

When a user finds an item, they will be able to make purchases via in-app cart and checkout services. Vendors who operate their own storefronts will have the option to additionally provide an external link to their storefronts operated elsewhere.

## Intended Audience

The intended audience is two targets:

- **Local businesses** — specifically those in a user area that are one-person teams or small groups with designs but too small to stand out on websites like Etsy.
- **Shoppers** — people looking for a unique way to discover pieces that match their personal style.

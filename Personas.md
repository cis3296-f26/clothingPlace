# Personas

## Maya — Style-Conscious Shopper

Maya, age 22, is a Temple University student who likes finding clothes that feel personal rather than mass-produced. She saves outfit inspiration on social media and browses marketplaces, but often spends too long searching through unrelated listings. She's interested in independent brands, yet usually discovers them by chance or through friends.

**Goals.** Maya wants to find distinctive pieces that fit her style and budget, ideally from brands nearby. She'd use a quick style quiz to get started, then browse recommendations and narrow them down by category, price, color, or size. She prefers buying directly in one place rather than being sent to a separate storefront to complete a purchase.

**Frustrations.** Large marketplaces bury small brands among countless listings, search results often don't reflect her personal style, and finding local brands takes effort. External checkout links can also interrupt the shopping experience.

**How ClothingPlace helps.** Build an initial style profile, discover relevant products from local brands, filter results herself, and buy through an in-app checkout.

## Jordan — Independent Clothing-Brand Owner

Jordan, age 29, designs and sells small-batch clothing under an independent label. Jordan manages most of the business alone, including product photos, descriptions, inventory, and social media promotion. The brand has a small but loyal following, but reaching people beyond that audience is difficult. Large marketplaces feel crowded, while running a standalone storefront means Jordan must bring in every visitor.

**Goals.** Jordan wants a simple way to list products where shoppers are already looking for independent clothing. Local discovery could help the brand reach nearby customers, and an optional link to the brand's existing storefront would be useful for shoppers who want to explore more.

**Frustrations.** Social media reach is unpredictable and requires constant promotion, a standalone store does not automatically attract shoppers, and large marketplaces make it hard for a new brand to stand out.

**How ClothingPlace helps.** Create a vendor profile, list products with relevant categories and style tags, include the brand's location, and reach shoppers through discovery and recommendations.

## Marcus — Fashion Content Creator

Marcus, age 23, is a college student in Philadelphia who enjoys fashion and creating content on social media. He recently started a TikTok series where he discovers and reviews underrated clothing brands, especially smaller businesses in his area. Although he enjoys introducing his followers to new styles, finding independent brands worth featuring can be time-consuming. He often relies on Instagram, TikTok, and recommendations from friends to discover new businesses.

**Goals.** Marcus wants an easier way to find unique clothing brands that he can feature in his videos. He would use ClothingPlace to discover local vendors, explore their products, and find clothing that fits different styles and budgets. Being able to view multiple independent brands in one place would help him discover new content ideas while also supporting smaller businesses. He could also purchase items directly through the application to review or feature in his videos.

**Frustrations.** Having to search through multiple social media platforms to find smaller brands, limited information about their products, and the difficulty of discovering businesses that don't already have a large following.

**How ClothingPlace helps.** Discover independent clothing brands, browse their products, and find unique pieces to share with his audience.

## Priya — Boutique Owner

Priya, age 38, owns a small boutique in South Philadelphia that sells her own line of everyday basics and workwear. She already runs an online store on Shopify, but almost all of its traffic comes from people who have visited her shop in person. She has tried paid social media ads, but they were expensive and brought in few repeat buyers.

**Goals.** Priya wants a second sales channel that brings her new local customers without rebuilding her whole online presence. She would list her best-selling pieces on ClothingPlace with accurate style tags and sizes, and add a link to her Shopify store so shoppers who like her brand can see her full catalog.

**Frustrations.** Her Shopify store does not bring in visitors on its own, ads cost more than they return, and keeping listings up to date on several sites takes time she does not have.

**How ClothingPlace helps.** Set up a vendor profile with her location and storefront link, list products quickly, and reach nearby shoppers whose style matches her brand.

## Denise — Gift Shopper

Denise, age 54, is a nurse who lives in the Philadelphia suburbs. She likes supporting local businesses and often buys clothing as gifts for her two college-age kids. She is comfortable shopping on her phone but finds large marketplaces overwhelming, and she is never sure what styles her kids actually like.

**Goals.** Denise wants an easy way to find local brands near her and browse by her kids' style rather than her own. She would filter by price and size to stay within her budget, save a few options, and compare them before buying. A simple cart and a clear order confirmation matter more to her than social features.

**Frustrations.** Cluttered search results, not knowing whether a brand is actually local, and checkout flows that send her to unfamiliar sites.

**How ClothingPlace helps.** Find nearby brands, filter by price and size, save items, and buy everything through one simple in-app checkout.

## Andre — New Philadelphia Resident

Andre, age 27, is a software developer who recently relocated to Philadelphia for a new job. He wants to feel connected to his new city and prefers clothing with a story behind it over big-box fast fashion. He doesn't know the local shopping scene yet, works long hours, and rarely has time to visit boutiques or weekend pop-up markets in person.

**Goals.** Andre wants a quick way to learn which independent brands are based in Philadelphia and what styles they offer. He would take the style quiz to set up his profile, then scroll through recommendations during his commute and save pieces he likes. He values knowing where a brand is located and what it represents, and would check a vendor's profile or external storefront before buying from a brand for the first time.

**Frustrations.** Not knowing where to start in an unfamiliar city, local brands being scattered across Instagram pages and in-person markets, and not having time to browse store by store.

**How ClothingPlace helps.** Discover nearby brands that match his style, learn about each vendor through their profile and location, save items to revisit later, and buy through a single in-app checkout.
